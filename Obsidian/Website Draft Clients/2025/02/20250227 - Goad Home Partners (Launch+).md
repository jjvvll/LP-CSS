==I've attached brand guidelines that I created a few months ago. I'm not completely tied to these fonts. For our brand think Adventure meets Luxury. A rugged, adventure driven style with a sophisticated natural aesthetic. Refined, yet Approachable. Earthly color palettes inspired by nature. We align ourselves with brands like YETI Coolers, Howler Brothers, Patagonia, and Free Fly Apparel. The color palette consists of earthy and coastal tones—deep navy, soft greens, and warm neutrals—evoking a sense of luxury, nature, and tranquility.== 

The mood board showcases serene landscapes, rustic architecture, and flowing water, reinforcing a connection to outdoor living and refined comfort. 

The typography combines Montserrat Bold and Brice SemiExpanded for headers, with Inter for paragraphs and Telegraf for subtitles, ensuring a modern and readable style. 

The mood board reflects a balance between nature, adventure, and high-end living, reinforcing the brand’s identity from "Marietta to the Mountains." 

We really want to evoke this sense of outdoor, fun, active living but also appealing to clients who fall into the luxury category. We believe we are unique in this approach to our brand and have an opportunity to appeal to those who are of high-net worth who favor an active lifestyle. We are a local expert in our community when it comes to mountain biking, camping, hiking, Onewheeling (Greg) and adventure travel. 

We previously lived out west in Big Sky, Montana so that is where a lot of our inspiration comes from - a love of the outdoors in luxury market.
