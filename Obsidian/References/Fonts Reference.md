The Burkett Team is a full-service real estate team serving Port St. Joe and Florida’s Forgotten Coast, built on experience, strategy, and client-first advocacy.


Led by Brian Burkett, a broker, owner, and agent with over 20 years in real estate, the team is known for skilled negotiations, market expertise, and protecting client equity. Brian works closely with sellers to maximize value and with buyers to secure strong, well-negotiated deals in competitive coastal markets.


Kristy Graves, Operations Director, brings nearly five years of real estate experience and a strong educational background to the team. She oversees valuations, transaction flow, follow-up systems, and day-to-day operations—ensuring every detail is handled and every client experience runs smoothly from contract to closing.


Margaret Buell-D’Ambrosi, Marketing Director, has been a licensed Realtor since 2017 in Georgia and 2024 in Florida. She brings over 20 years of experience in marketing, branding, photography, and videography. She leads strategic marketing for every listing, combining professional visuals, digital advertising, and targeted campaigns to help homes stand out and sell with purpose.


Together, The Burkett Team offers expert guidance for buyers, sellers, and investors across Port St. Joe, St. Joe Beach, Mexico Beach, Cape San Blas, and surrounding coastal communities—delivering thoughtful strategy, strong advocacy, and a seamless real estate experience from start to finish.
